Quality and credible claims

Face, product and production

Keep the creator on camera throughout the entire video; open and close with the creator and mask together. Keep the full face visible during explanations; during wear, show the mask and lower face. For detail shots, use a face-visible split-screen or keep the creator in frame.

Record sharp product details, steady B-roll, clear English speech. Avoid mirrored logos, dark unreadable fabric, exaggerated reactions and heavy filters.

Credible demonstrations

Describe real experience, including limitations. Describe only your actual use, travel and observations. Do not promise treatment, instant sleep, zero grogginess, noise cancellation or equal comfort for everyone.

Follow the sample instructions for fit, warmth and care. Use the mask only while safely resting. For flight footage, follow crew instructions and filming rules, and protect other travelers’ privacy.
